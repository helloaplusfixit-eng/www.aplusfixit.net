Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aplus verify its professionals? ▼
Every pro goes through a 4-step process: criminal background check, license verification, insurance confirmation, and a skills assessment. Only those who pass all four earn the Aplus Verified badge.
Is Aplus Fixit available in my city? ▼
We're launching in our first city soon! Join the waitlist and you'll be notified the moment we go live in your area. Early waitlist members get $20 off their first booking.
How much does it cost to use Aplus? ▼
Joining the waitlist is completely free. When we launch, booking a pro is free — you only pay for the service itself. Pricing varies by service type and pro, and is always shown upfront before you confirm.
What if I'm not happy with the work? ▼
We stand behind every job. If you're not satisfied, contact us and we'll work to make it right — whether that means a redo, a refund, or finding you a new pro.
I'm a contractor. How do I join Aplus? ▼
We'd love to have you! Apply through our provider sign-up form (launching soon). Founding providers get zero commission for their first 30 days and priority placement in search results.
